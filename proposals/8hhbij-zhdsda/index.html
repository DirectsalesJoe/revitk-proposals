Context

Where We Are

Vohkus is scaling to 35 quota-carrying reps. Neo is onboarding 2-3 experienced hires a month, each with relationships to bring across and a £150k GP year-one target. These people can sell. They need to sell the Vohkus way, into the Vohkus portfolio, quickly.

Historically, around 75% of new starters haven't made it through the ramp. There's no single documented path that takes someone from signed contract to confidently positioning Vohkus, asking the right questions, and opening a services conversation. Each induction starts from scratch.

The established team hasn't had a formal sales refresh in years. New packaged services are being built, but the enablement to sell them doesn't exist yet. The emphasis is shifting to a solution-focused, problem-centric approach that drives earlier engagement with specialists and more consultative conversations. MEDDPICC is in the CRM, but adoption and understanding across the organisation is inconsistent. The framework needs to be embedded into how reps actually sell day to day, not just how they update Salesforce.

Salesforce, LinkedIn Sales Nav, Cognism, and CoPilot are all licensed. The technology is there. The consensus across the team is that most reps aren't using AI to drive efficiencies, and those who are have had no formal training on how to get the most out of it.

This is a behaviour change challenge, not a one-off training event. New starters, the established team, and the specialists all need consistent reinforcement over time for any of this to stick.

"We've got all these tools, but nobody's shown us how to actually use them."
The Opportunity

What AI Can Do Right Now

With what you already own. No new platforms. No new budget.

Right now, if a rep asks CoPilot to write a cold call script, they'll get a generic cold call script. It has no context. It doesn't know what a good Vohkus M-Drive call sounds like. It doesn't know the portfolio, the objections, or the personas.

That's the gap. The technology works. It just hasn't been given the right instructions.

With the right prompts and context, CoPilot can already do this inside your existing M365 estate:

  • Pre-meeting briefing. Account overview, recent news, technology footprint, and suggested talking points specific to Vohkus. 45 minutes of research compressed to 5.
  • Follow-up emails. Draft a positioned follow-up from Teams meeting notes, referencing the buyer's specific situation and agreed next steps. 20 minutes becomes 2.
  • Cold outreach. Targeted M-Drive emails by persona, technology, and value proposition. First draft in 30 seconds.
  • Discovery questions. "5 questions for an IT Director at a mid-market manufacturer on Cisco networking coming up for renewal." Specific, relevant, ready to use.
  • Technology positioning. "Why should a logistics company with 200 users and ageing on-prem Exchange be interested in a workspace migration?" Builds the business case the rep walks in with.
  • Proposal drafting. First-draft proposal sections generated from meeting notes. The rep edits and positions rather than writing from scratch.
  • Sales coaching. Drop a call transcript in and see exactly what questions were asked, what was missed, and where the conversation quality sits. Objective, immediate feedback without sitting on every call.

None of this requires new technology, new platforms, or new budget. It runs on CoPilot, inside the M365 estate you already pay for.
