Context

Where We Are

35 quota-carrying reps. 2-3 experienced hires arriving every month. Each with a £150k GP year-one target and relationships to bring across. They can sell. They need to sell the Vohkus way, quickly.

75% of new starters historically haven't made it through the ramp. No single documented path from signed contract to confidently positioning Vohkus. Each induction starts from scratch.

No formal sales refresh for the established team in years. Packaged services being built, but no enablement to sell them yet. MEDDPICC is in the CRM. Adoption across the organisation is patchy. The framework needs to live in how reps sell, not just how they update Salesforce.

Salesforce, Sales Nav, Cognism, CoPilot: all licensed. Most reps aren't using AI. Those who are have had no training on how to get the most out of it.

"We've got all these tools, but nobody's shown us how to actually use them."
The Opportunity

What AI Can Do Right Now

Ask CoPilot to write a cold call script today and you'll get a generic cold call script. No context. No knowledge of M-Drive, the portfolio, the objections, or the personas.

The technology works. It just hasn't been given the right instructions. With the right prompts, CoPilot already does this inside your M365 estate:

  • Pre-meeting briefing. Account overview, recent news, tech footprint, talking points. 45 minutes compressed to 5.
  • Follow-up emails. Positioned follow-up from Teams meeting notes. 20 minutes becomes 2.
  • Cold outreach. M-Drive emails by persona, technology, and value prop. First draft in 30 seconds.
  • Discovery questions. "5 questions for an IT Director at a manufacturer on Cisco networking coming up for renewal." Ready to use.
  • Technology positioning. "Why should a logistics company with 200 users and ageing on-prem Exchange care about a workspace migration?" The business case, ready to walk in with.
  • Proposal drafting. First-draft sections from meeting notes. Edit and send.
  • Sales coaching. Drop a call transcript in. See what questions were asked, what was missed, where the quality sits. Immediate feedback.

No new technology. No new platforms. No new budget.

Before Wednesday

Pre-Session Cheat Sheet

Quick answers to these before Wednesday will save us time on the day. If you're not sure, flag it and we'll cover it live.

For IT (Alex)

  1. What Microsoft 365 plan is the sales team on?
  2. Is Microsoft 365 CoPilot licensed? If so, how many users have it, and is it available to all sales reps or specific teams only?
  3. Are there any CoPilot features that have been restricted or switched off by IT?
  4. Are there any group-level policies around AI tools that we need to be aware of?
  5. What's the current setup for Teams meeting transcription and recording? Enabled, restricted, or switched off?
  6. Is there anything in the admin centre that would prevent us deploying custom prompt templates for the sales team?

For Salesforce (Raj)

  1. What Salesforce edition is Vohkus on?
  2. Is Salesforce Einstein active? If so, which features are turned on?
  3. MEDDPICC: configured as fields in opportunities? Which get filled in consistently and which tend to be left blank?
  4. Where do account plans live? Salesforce, SharePoint, or somewhere else?
  5. Any integration between Salesforce and CoPilot currently, or used separately?
  6. What are you currently building or planning with Einstein? We want to complement, not duplicate.

For Legal/Compliance (Hannah)

  1. Is there an existing AI usage policy or acceptable use document?
  2. What are reps currently allowed to use CoPilot for, and is anything explicitly off limits?
  3. What's the position on Teams call recording and transcription for customer-facing meetings?
  4. If we want to expand CoPilot usage into new areas, what's the approval process?

For Marketing (Carmen)

  1. Is there a brand or messaging approval process for outbound sales communications?
  2. Are there existing email templates or messaging guidelines that AI-generated content should follow?

For Sales Leaders (Neo, Simon, Charles, Jen)

  1. What's the single biggest time drain for your reps each week that you'd want AI to handle?
  2. Which tools does your team use most: CoPilot, Cognism, Sales Nav, or something else?
  3. If you could give every rep one new skill or capability tomorrow, what would it be?
  4. How would you rate your team's AI confidence? Roughly what percentage falls into each group?
