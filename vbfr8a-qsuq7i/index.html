Before we build: your questions, answered

The things people ask us most, answered straight, so there are no surprises before we even meet.

Everything we do, and the way we do it, comes down to delivering a quality finish at a price we stand behind, with workmanship we're proud of and no nasty surprises. Here's what that means in practice.

Can you do my job, and what will it cost?

Have you done jobs like mine, and how experienced are you?

Almost certainly. Tom and Jake have both been in the trade since they were sixteen, so that's twenty years each. We run a core team of five, with around fifteen tradesmen who have worked with us for years. You get the same faces on your job from the first day to the last.

You're not the cheapest though, are you?

No, and we're upfront about that. We're a quality, fixed price builder, not the lowest quote on the street. If you get three quotes we'll usually come in around the middle. Where we're different is that we give you options, so you can shape the spec to your budget without dropping the quality of the build.

It's a fixed price. What if it ends up costing more than your quote?

Simply put, it won't. Our price is our price. We're meticulous in the planning, so you'll never get a phone call halfway through the job asking for more money. That's the whole point of how we work.

Has that ever happened?

Yes, though not often. We recently finished a job in Bedford that cost us about £7,000 more than we'd quoted. We'd fixed the price, so we took the hit. There's always a contingency built into our quote for exactly this, and the truth is we'd lose money on a job before we'd lose our reputation.

How you're protected

Do I get a warranty or guarantee?

Yes. We give you a two year guarantee on all our workmanship, on top of the manufacturers' warranties on the products we fit. If anything isn't right, we come back and put it right.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry £5 million of public liability as standard. If you'd like extra peace of mind we can add insolvency cover on top, so that even in the unlikely event anything happened to the company, the cost of finishing your build is covered.

How the build runs

How much disruption will there be?

Most of the build happens outside your living space, so day to day it's more manageable than people expect. The main disruption is when we knock through, which is two to three weeks of some dust, and we plan that around you and the family to keep it contained.

Do I need an architect, and what does that cost?

If you don't already have drawings and planning in place, then yes. We work closely with an architect we trust, and his drawings are excellent. He'll price the drawings, usually three to four thousand pounds, and handle the design and planning side. We stay alongside you throughout and bring it through to your build quote.

How long does it all take?

If you need drawings, they take around two to three weeks, and planning can take seven to twelve weeks. The build itself is usually two to four months, depending on the job. We map the whole timeline for you at the start, so you always know what's coming next.

How does payment work?

We take a 10% deposit once you accept the job. If it doesn't get through planning we refund that, minus the architect's fee, and we keep going until it does. After that you pay in stages against building control sign-offs, usually around six milestones on an extension. There are no large sums up front.

A few others people ask

What if I spend more than it adds to the house?

That depends what you're after, and we'll be straight with you about it. Some clients want to stay within what the work adds in value. Others are staying put for years and are building for how they want to live, not for resale. We'll give you options either way.

Why are you now Heritage Build Group?

We changed the name because the work had outgrown the old one. We've been building for over twenty years, and Heritage reflects the quality we deliver now. It's the same team and the same standards, with a name that finally matches the work.

Can you help with funding?

Yes. We can arrange funding through Phoenix Finance, often at preferential rates, so the project can start when you're ready.

If there's anything here you'd like to talk through, that's exactly what the first visit is for. We'll walk through your space, understand what you're after, and get you to a quote that's right for you, and one we'll stand behind.
